Elasticat FM-1

A loop mangler for the FM-1 panel: six tracks of loops, slices and grains, a 128-step sequencer with parameter locks, a 128-slot sample pool, effects, modulation and scenes. It is a rewrite of the norns script Elasticat.

Elasticat is a Fantasy CFW: it runs in the RemoteFM1 hosts (the web player, the iOS and Android apps, the FM1VST plug-in), never on an FM-1 by itself. The FM-1 is its panel. It needs more memory than the hardware has and it reads your samples from the RemoteFM1 folder.

Everything here describes version 0.6.15. Names in CAPS are what the panel's labels and the screen say.

Setting up

  1. Open a RemoteFM1 host (the site, an app or the plug-in), pick Elasticat from the catalog and give it your RemoteFM1 folder when it asks.
  2. Put WAV files in that folder's audio/ (subfolders are fine). Elasticat reads them from there; it never moves or writes them.
  3. Projects, takes and recordings go to data/elasticat/ in the same folder: projects/ holds your .ecp project files, rec/ the SAMPLER's takes.
  4. A file named with its tempo, like break_bpm136.wav, is tagged with it on load (BPM 136). Untagged files get the project's tempo; you can set it in the EDITOR.

Power-on plays a short intro (any key skips it; APP SETTINGS turns it off) and lands in LOOP ENGINE with the last project loaded.

The panel

Elasticat uses the FM-1's names from the FIZZ layout: white keys W1…W16, black keys B1…B11, the knobs SEL, ALGO, PRESETS, K1…K4 and MASTER. GLO is SHIFT, OCT- is F1, OCT+ is F2, and the button printed SAVE is FILE.

Buttons and modes

Each menu button opens a mode. A quick click on a button whose mode is already showing cycles its sub-modes. A hold on any menu button turns the black keys into a mode picker: the button's modes light on B1, B2, B3…, the header says PICK A MODE, and a black key opens that one (which also becomes the button's own). Letting go without a key changes nothing.

B11 and the pages

On every track page (LOOP ENGINE, TRIG, KEYBOARD, FILTER, AMP, FX, MODULATION) B11 held is a layer of its own:

  • the white keys select steps of the shown page (keys held together are one selection; let them all go and the next press starts a new one);
  • B1…B8 are the pattern's pages, FIZZ style: tap = go to the page, hold = mute it (out of the loop), double-tap = solo it, two keys held = loop between them (past the end: the track grows to that page);
  • the cells area shows the pages panel, with K1 = LEN (pattern length), K2 = RATE, K3 = the loop-key zoom;
  • F1 clears the selected steps, F2 goes LIVE (clears the selection).

A quick click on B11 latches the step layer (click again to go back). On FILTER, AMP, FX and MOD the quick click keeps the page's own B11 function (the envelope mode, FX MIX, MACROS). Patterns have up to 128 steps = 8 pages.

Colours and LEDs

Blue = LIVE. The step strip is blue when nothing is selected: the loop keys play live and, in LOOP ENGINE, set the track's loop.

Red = loop keys with steps selected: what you play on the keys is written into those steps.

Yellow = the STEP layer (B11): the keys select steps.

White = the selected steps, on every layer. Under a step, a short bar in the lock colour is its region lock.

Orange = the playhead, the accent, alerts.

Key LEDs follow the layer: on the loop keys only the active loop range is lit and the selected steps blink; on the step layer trigs are mid, empty steps dark, the selection blinks. A held menu button lights only its picker keys. The header's title line shows messages (STOP FIRST, SAVED, PAGE 2/4) and what a held key does.

Quick start: a loop in two minutes

1. Load a sound

  1. Press FILE. You are in BROWSE, in audio/.
  2. SEL moves the cursor; B2 enters a folder, B1 goes up. Each move previews the file once (turn the preview off with PRESETS).
  3. Hold white keys to hear parts of the file as loop keys.
  4. B10 (or ALGO clockwise) adds it to the pool; the selected track plays it.

2. Play it live

  1. Press HOME (LOOP ENGINE) and PLAY.
  2. The white keys are 16 loop keys over the sample: hold one to loop that slice, two to span them. Let go and the playhead returns.
  3. Turn K1…K4 (PITCH, SLOT, START, END on the LOOP page). B3 is the WARP page: try GRAIN.

3. Sequence it

  1. Press HOME again for TRIG. Tap white keys to put trigs on steps.
  2. Hold a step and turn a knob: that value is locked on the step.
  3. Back in LOOP ENGINE, turn ALGO to select a step (its column goes white), then play the loop keys: the region you play is written into that step.

4. More tracks, then save

  1. SHIFT + W2 selects track 2 (or MIX's keys). Load it a sound and repeat.
  2. MIX: tap a track key to select, hold it to mute.
  3. SHIFT + FILE saves the project. It also saves itself when stopped.

Getting sounds in

Samples live in the pool: 128 slots sharing 200 MiB (about 20 minutes of stereo). A track plays its slot (the SLOT parameter on LOOP ENGINE's first page); any step can lock a different slot.

Sequencing

Each of the six tracks has its own pattern (length, rate and 128 steps) in each of 64 patterns, A1 to D16. The selected track is the one the keys and knobs edit: pick it with SHIFT + W1–W6, SHIFT + PRESETS, or in MIX.

LOOP ENGINE (HOME)

The track's sample, its machine and warp, and the loop keys.

B1 LOOP, B2 MACHINE, B3 WARP, B4 RANGEthe pages. Tapped again, a key steps the page's lanes. Held, B1 picks the loop mode, B2 the machine, B3 the warp mode, B4 the voice mode (MONO / POLY) from a list; turn SEL or K1 while holding

The waveform shows the loop range; the brackets are the track's region (static while playing) and the bright span is what the engine plays now. With the GRAIN warp each grain is drawn where it reads, with a blue head at its leading end. Slice machines turn the white keys into velocity-sensitive slice pads.

Machines (B2 held): LOOP, TRIG (one-shot), SLICE and S.POLY (even slices), RAZOR and R.POLY (the SLICE editor's table, mono / 8 voices), NEIGH (the track to the left), INPUT (the live input). Warps (B3 held, stopped): TAPE, VARI, DOMINO, GRAIN, OLA, PITCH, HARM, WTBL, FREEZE, FORMNT, GSTR, GSTR2, CHOP.

TRIG (HOME again)

The step grid. A quick tap on a white key puts a trig on the step or takes it off. Hold a step (or select steps with B11) and turn a knob to lock a value. The strip shows trigs, ghosts (lock-only steps), a VELOCITY lock as the trig's height and a PITCH lock as a blue mark. Pages: TRIG (B1), COND (B2: chance, ratchets, conditions), PLAYHEAD (B3: jump and return per step). With a step held, the black keys set its pitch: B6 is 0, B1 is −5 semitones, B11 is +5.

KEYBOARD (HOME again)

The keybed plays the selected track chromatically (C4 on W5; F1 / F2 move the octave). Stopped, a held key plays the loop at that pitch. With a step selected (ALGO), a key writes a note on that step and moves on to the next (ADVANCE in LOOP SETUP; SHIFT + key does the other). On slice tracks B1–B10 pick the slice and a quick click on B11 steps the slice bank. The sounding key fills in the mode's colour; a selected step's note shows white.

Locks, copy and undo

  • Locks stick as on an Elektron: a locked value holds until a later trig without that lock puts the track value back. SLIDE (TRIG, lane 2) glides between locked values.
  • Hold a step + PRESETS locks a pool slot on it: that step plays another sample.
  • F1 + a knob clears that parameter's locks (on the held steps, else the whole track); F2 + a knob clears its modulation.
  • Copy / cut / paste: with F1 (copy) or F2 (cut) on, tap a step to take it into the buffer, tap another to paste. The same works on whole pages (PAGES, black keys) and patterns (PATTERN LAUNCH, white keys). The buffer empties when F1 / F2 turns off.
  • SHIFT + F1 / F2 undo and redo, gesture by gesture, with the gesture's name in the header.

Live recording (REC)

REC records what you play on the selected track while the transport runs: KEYBOARD notes (pitch and velocity locked), loop keys (the loop position locked), slice pads, and knob moves as locks (AUTOMATION). Hits land on the nearest step and keep their offset as a MICRO lock. Hold REC for the settings: MODE (CAPTURE starts playback on the first key; FIXED records a set number of bars; OVERDUB adds), COUNT-IN, METRONOME, CLICK LVL, AUTOMATION, QUANTIZE.

Shaping the sound

Each track runs: sample → FILTER → INSERT 1 → INSERT 2 → AMP (level, pan) → the mix, with sends to the S1 and S2 buses and the master insert after that. FILTER (B1 the type held, B2 its envelope, B3 mix) and AMP (B1 the envelope mode held, B2 AMP ENV, B3 MOD ENV) are the SEL and ENV buttons. All their values lock on steps.

Modulation

On every track page the black keys B6–B10 are the sources: LFO 1, LFO 2, MOD ENV, VEL, AT. Hold a source and turn any value knob: the first detent makes the route, the next set its depth. F2 held while turning removes it. Steps held as well make depth locks. The MOD button (MODULATION) has the MATRIX (B1: eight slots, SEL the row, K1 source, K2 destination, K3 depth), the LFO pages (B6, B7), VELOCITY (B9), and the four macros M1–M4 (B2–B5, overview on B11), which are sources too and lockable values.

FX

The FX button: B1 / B2 select INSERT 1 / INSERT 2 (tap again for their knob pages; hold for the effect type), B3 / B4 the send buses, B5 steps SENDS (the routing picture: K1 and K2 the inserts' MIX, K3 and K4 the send levels, ALGO the send tap PRE / POST) and MASTER (the master effect and the load meter), B11 the FX MIX page. Eighteen effects: DELAY, REVERB, LOFI, COMP, DESTROY, ECHO, BLACKHOLE, CHORUS, FLANGER, PHASER, DJ EQ, DUCK, TAPE ECHO, CASSETTE, MOTION, RINGS, LIMIT. On SENDS, hold an effect's key and turn K1 to move it to the other slot.

Arranging

MIX and PAGES (EDIT)

MIX: W1–W9 are T1–T6, S1, S2, MST. Tap to select, hold to mute, SHIFT + key to solo. B1–B8 set the track's rate (1/8 … 4X), B9 / B10 step it, B11 is the CAT (the dancing-cat visualiser). PRESETS selects the track. The knobs are LEVEL, PAN, SEND 1, SEND 2 (MASTER: BPM, SWING, GLO LENGTH).

PAGES: the black keys are the step pages with the same gestures as B11's (tap, hold to mute, double-tap to solo, two held to loop). K1 LEN, K2 RATE, PRESETS the page; the loop reads in the header (LOOP ALL, LOOP 1-2). F1 / F2 copy and cut whole pages.

F3 (SHIFT + SEQ, or F1 + F2): the timing menu with SEL tempo, ALGO swing, PRESETS to name the pattern, K1 / K2 the track's LENGTH and RATE, K3 / K4 the global length and the launch quantize.

PATTERN LAUNCH (SEQ)

B1–B4 pick the bank A–D; the white keys launch the bank's 16 patterns on the launch quantize (at once when stopped). With F1 / F2 on, the white keys copy, cut and paste whole patterns. The playing pattern's LED is bright, the queued one blinks.

CROSSFADER (ARP)

Two scenes, A and B, and a fader between them. Hold B1 or B2 and turn a knob to put that value into the scene; B11 held + B1 / B2 captures the current values into the scene (those that differ from their default, or that the other scene holds); SHIFT + B1 / B2 clears a scene. W1–W12 are the fader: hold a key and it glides there, harder pressure glides faster; SHIFT + key jumps. W13–W16 set the base glide time. K1 is the fader as a knob. The fader is never recorded; a firing step's lock wins over it.

Reference

The header

Left: the target (T1 … MST). Middle: the mode and page, or a message, or what a held key will do (shown after a quarter second so a quick tap never flashes it; sticky SHIFT / F1 / F2 show at once). Right: the page number, the selected step (S09), or F1 / F2 while sticky.

Hold menus

LOOP ENGINE's B1–B5 and FX's B1–B5 open a list while held; SEL or K1 moves the cursor and letting go picks. A quick tap still opens the key's page.
